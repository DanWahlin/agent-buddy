# Grok

A rendition of xAI's Grok bot for Grok Build: a white ball with two black pill
eyes, on black like every other character. It uses the `base-patch` layout, the
same as Copilot and Claude.

All 13 tracks are **rendered** from a small geometric model, not generated by an
image model, so every pose is registered by construction. The look follows the
Grok bot (a ball whose slanted pill eyes slide over its surface, squash to a dash
when they close, and go round when startled), but the geometry is this
renderer's own: it uses no assets from xAI's apps.

## Regenerating

```bash
python3 characters/grok/tools/render_grok.py     # 1,440 cells, contact sheets; about 2 minutes
python3 characters/grok/tools/export_frames.py   # frames.bin + frames.json
python3 tools/character_pack.py build            # build/characters/grok.acpk
npm run character grok                           # install over USB or Wi-Fi
```

The steps are deterministic. CI re-runs the export and fails if `frames.bin` or
`frames.json` would change. The pack builder runs `validate_frames.py`, which uses
only the standard library and refuses a `frames.bin` that no longer matches the
committed cells. The renderer and the exporter need `requirements-art.txt`.

## What is here

| Path | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `sprites/` | **The art.** 8 idle and 5 expression tracks (24 poses, 12 for `up` and `down`) x 5 blink levels: 1,440 grayscale cells of 412x352, a manifest per track with each pose's parameters, and the merged `animation.json`. |
| `preview/` | A contact sheet per track and one of the blink levels, built with the cells. |
| `frames.bin`, `frames.json` | The exported pack payload and its frame table. |
| `tools/render_grok.py` | The model and renderer, and every track's poses. |
| `tools/export_frames.py` | The base-patch exporter. |
| `tools/validate_frames.py` | The pack builder's check that `frames.bin` still matches `sprites/`. |

## The model

- **The ball** is a sphere seen straight on, radius 146 px, at the center of the
  character canvas. Turning it never changes the silhouette, so only the eyes
  move. The idle looks also shift the ball up to 7 px toward where it looks.
- **Each eye** is a decal: a pill (stadium) drawn in the tangent plane at its
  center on the sphere and projected back onto the surface, so it foreshortens
  as it slides toward the edge. Eyes stop short of the edge (`RIM_DEPTH`), so a
  black eye never runs into the black background and breaks the outline.
- **Cells are drawn at the device's size** with 4x4 supersampling. Copilot and
  Claude cells are 240x224 and are scaled up on export; at that scale a crisp
  white edge goes soft, so Grok skips the resampling.
- **The eyes are pure black**, the background color, so they read as cut out of
  the ball, as the Grok bot's do. Effects stay out of them because they are inside
  the face area that effects avoid, and the desktop keeps them because the ball
  encloses them.

## Tracks

| Track | Last pose |
| --- | --- |
| `right`, `left` | Turned 40 degrees |
| `up`, `down` | Pitched 24 degrees |
| diagonals | 30 degrees of turn and 18 of pitch |
| `surprise` | A spring: the ball stretches and hops, the eyes go big and round, then everything settles back to neutral on the last pose |
| `working` | Flat upper lids come down while it looks a little lower: focused |
| `complete` | The eyes close to dashes, then bend into happy arcs as the ball lifts |
| `attention`, `attention_alternate` | A 12-degree questioning tilt to each side, one eye grown and the other shrunk |

Sleep needs no track: the motion engine closes the neutral pose's eyes.

## Blinks

Blink levels are `[1, 0.75, 0.5, 0.25, 0]`. Each level is re-rendered: the pill
gets shorter, then round, then a wide oval, and closes to a short dash along the
eye line. A closed eye keeps the dash; an eye that disappears reads as a blank
face. The largest blink patch is 7,200 pixels, under Copilot's 7,452.

## Gotchas

- **Keep the eye line level.** In sleep, the device peeks by showing the next
  blink level in rows that grow from the middle of the eye patch. With the head
  tilted, the two eyes sit at different heights, the band cuts them unevenly, and
  the peek looks jagged. The Grok lean is in each eye's own slant (`EYE_SLANT`),
  not in a head roll. The turns roll the head by up to 3°, but in sleep the head
  turns only 12 to 22% of the way to the right or left, so the two eyes stay
  within one pixel of the same height. The attention tracks tilt the head by 12°
  and run with normal blinks.
- **Frame 0 must be the same image on every track**, and `surprise` must end on
  it. The renderer and the exporter both check this.
