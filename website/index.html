Your agent is done. Another needs your attention. Did you notice?

Agents work in the background, in terminals and windows you are not looking at. Agent Buddy sits on your desk and tells you what they are doing, at a glance.

It reacts to your agents

Busy, waiting, or done. At a glance.

Agent Buddy shows what each agent is doing, and a small badge shows which agent. When your agents are quiet, it looks around, reacts when you tap it, and takes a nap.

Working

An agent is busy. Your buddy is busy too: focused eyes, orbiting dots, and drifting binary digits.

Needs attention

An agent is waiting for your approval. A curious head tilt and an amber question mark tell you now, not ten minutes later.

Complete

The work is done. A short celebration, then back to idle. Come take a look.

    Desktop Agent Buddy

    No device? Use your desktop.

    The same character, with the same animation, in a small transparent window. Put it beside your editor, your terminal, or on a second screen. Use it with the device, or on its own.

    • The device's own animation. Not a copy. The app runs the firmware's code, and a parity test checks every frame pixel for pixel.
    • Follows the device. The same state and agent badges, from the same companion service.
    • Your choice of look. Show the case around the character, or only the character, straight on your desktop.
    • Optional sounds. The device's sound cues, through your computer.

    Make it yours.

    A local Settings page for characters, agents, badges, Wi-Fi, and the desktop app. Press the BOOT button on the device to open it.

    How it works.

    Three small pieces, all on your computer and your desk.

    Hooks

    Setup adds a hook to each agent you have. It reports events such as “started a tool”, “needs permission”, and “finished”.

    Companion service

    Runs in the background. It combines the events into one state, sends it to the device, and serves Settings.

    Your buddy

    The device, over USB or local Wi-Fi, or the desktop app. Hooks only notify: your agents keep working if your buddy is off.

    Get started.

    Everything you need is free and on GitHub. Pick the way that fits your desk.

    With a device

    Waveshare ESP32-S3-Touch-AMOLED-1.75

    The B or C model. A 1.75-inch round AMOLED touch screen.

    1. Download the desktop app and open it.
    2. Connect the device with a USB data cable.
    3. In Settings, open Device and select Install.
    4. Pick a character. Connect Wi-Fi to go untethered.

    For developers

    Run it from source.

    Needs Node.js 24.11 or newer. Setup installs the agent hooks, starts the service, and opens Settings.

    git clone https://github.com/DanWahlin/agent-buddy.git
cd agent-buddy
npm run setup
